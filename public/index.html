Our story

The Pearl City has always smelled of the sea.

Thoothukudi was once known to the world for its pearls. People here still call it முத்துநகர், Muthu Nagar. Long before refrigeration, families along this coast kept the catch the only way they knew: salt, turmeric and days of hot sun.

We still do it that way. Our fish comes straight off the boats at dawn and is cleaned and salted on the same day. It dries on bamboo racks a few metres from the waves. Nothing is added that your grandmother wouldn't recognise.

How it's made

From the boat to your kitchen

  1. 5:00 am

    The dawn catch

    Country boats return to the Thoothukudi harbour. We pick only the freshest fish, firm and bright-eyed.

  2. 8:00 am

    Clean & salt

    Each fish is gutted, washed in clean water and rubbed with sea salt and a little turmeric, which is a natural preservative.

  3. Days 1–3

    Sun on the racks

    The fish dries on raised racks in the coastal sun and sea breeze. It's turned by hand and never touches the sand.

  4. Day 4

    Seal & ship

    We sort it and vacuum-seal it in odour-proof packs, then ship it to you. Your neighbours won't know.

In the kitchen

Three ways to fall in love

15 min

Nethili fry

Soak in warm water for 10 minutes, then drain. Fry with shallots, chilli powder and curry leaves until crisp. It goes perfectly with rasam rice.

35 min

Karuvadu kuzhambu

Make a tangy tamarind gravy with brinjal, drumstick and garlic. Add the karuvadu in the last 10 minutes. It tastes even better the next day.

20 min

Sura puttu

Boil and flake the dried shark. Sauté it with onion, garlic, turmeric and grated coconut. It's a Thoothukudi classic.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does karuvadu last?

Unopened vacuum packs keep for 3 months in a cool, dry place. After opening, store it in an airtight box in the fridge and it will last up to 6 months.

Will the parcel smell?

No. Every pack is vacuum-sealed and double-bagged in odour-proof packaging. You'll only smell it when you open it.

Where do you deliver and how much does it cost?

We deliver across India. Enter your pincode at checkout to see the exact charge and delivery time. Tamil Nadu orders usually arrive in 2–4 days. Delivery is free on larger orders.

How do I pay?

Pay instantly with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) by scanning the QR code shown after you order. Cash on delivery is also available in most areas.

Is it very salty? How do I prepare it?

We use only as much salt as drying needs. Before cooking, soak it in warm water for 10–15 minutes and rinse twice. That removes the extra salt and softens the fish.
